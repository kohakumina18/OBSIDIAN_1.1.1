

# 📄 Stakeholder Communication in BA – From Planning to Trust (Decision-Driven Framework)

---

🧠 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Communication failure is **#1 reason projects fail**
    
- Effective communication = **Planning + Soft Skills**
    
- Key success factor:
    
    - right message
        
    - right stakeholder
        
    - right method
        
- Poor communication ≠ wrong words  
    → often wrong **channel / timing / audience**
    
- Trust is the foundation:
    
    ```text
    Trust = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-interest
    ```
    

---

🔍 KEY INSIGHTS / PROBLEMS

---

🔥 Core misconception

```text
Communication = talking ❌
Communication = system (who + what + how + when) ✅
```

---

🔥 Why projects fail

- no communication plan
    
- wrong stakeholders
    
- wrong method
    
- no feedback loop
    

👉 Insight:

```text
Unplanned communication = random outcomes
```

---

🔥 Critical mistake

```text
“I sent it → they understand” ❌
```

👉 Reality:

```text
No response = communication failure (your responsibility)
```

---

🔥 Hidden complexity

- stakeholders ≠ same
    
- preferences differ
    
- influence differs
    
- engagement differs

📦 STRUCTURED CONTENT

---

🧠 1. COMMUNICATION = 2 SYSTEMS

---

1. Planning (structure)
    

- who
    
- what
    
- when
    
- how
    

---

2. Soft skills (execution)
    

- listening
    
- empathy
    
- clarity
    
- trust
    

---

👉 Insight:

```text
Planning decides success
Soft skills deliver success
```

---

---

🧠 2. COMMUNICATION PLANNING FRAMEWORK

---

Core questions:

```text
WHO → stakeholder
WHAT → message
HOW → method
WHEN → timing
WHY → objective
```

---

👉 Minimal viable plan:

- can be 1-page grid
    
- does NOT need 50-page document
    

---

---

🧠 3. STAKEHOLDER ANALYSIS (CRITICAL)

---

Matrix:

```text
Influence vs Impact
```

---

Key insight:

```text
High impact + low influence = MOST IMPORTANT (often ignored)
```

👉 Example:

- end users
    
- operators
    
- warehouse staff
    

---

👉 BA role:

```text
Be their voice
```

---

---

🧠 4. COMMUNICATION MATRIX (CORE TOOL)

---

Structure:

- stakeholder
    
- purpose
    
- frequency
    
- method
    
- owner
    

---

👉 Critical factor:

```text
Method selection = success or failure
```

---

Example:

```text
Wrong:
Zoom + Workshop + PowerPoint ❌

Right:
Zoom + Workshop + Structured tool (Excel / system) ✅
```

---

👉 Insight:

```text
Tool must support thinking, not just presentation
```

---

---

🧠 5. COMMUNICATION MODEL (VERY IMPORTANT)

---

Flow:

```text
Sender → Message → Channel → Receiver → Feedback
```

---

Failure happens when:

- wrong channel
    
- unclear message
    
- environment noise
    
- no feedback
    

---

👉 Critical insight:

```text
You control MOST of the communication success
```

---

---

🧠 6. INFORMATION DESIGN (HIGH IMPACT SKILL)

---

Problem:

- stakeholders don’t read long text
    

---

Solution:

```text
Design information for scanning
```

---

Techniques:

- tables
    
- bullet points
    
- grouping
    
- highlights
    

---

---

Example:

---

❌ Bad:

- long paragraph
    

---

✅ Good:

```text
Task | Deadline | Owner
```

---

👉 Result:

```text
Faster understanding
Less error
Better response
```

---

---

🧠 7. REQUIREMENT CLARITY (CRITICAL)

---

Example transformation:

---

Before:

```text
Agents see all calls → want to change
```

---

After:

```text
Current: see all calls
Future: see only own calls
Change: restrict access
```

---

👉 Insight:

```text
Structured thinking reveals missing logic
```

---

---

🧠 8. STAKEHOLDER DISCOVERY

---

Technique:

```text
“Who else should I talk to?”
```

---

👉 Also:

- mind mapping
    
- SME input
    
- iterative discovery
    

---

👉 Insight:

```text
You NEVER know all stakeholders upfront
```

---

🧠 9. COMMUNICATION EFFICIENCY (VERY PRACTICAL)

---

Examples:

- use stakeholder preferred channel
    
- align time zones
    
- use visuals instead of text
    
- remove “communication waste”
    

---

👉 Insight:

```text
Good communication = Lean communication
```

---

---

🧠 10. TRUST (CORE FOUNDATION)

---

Formula:

```text
Trust = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-interest
```

---

Meaning:

- credibility → truthful
    
- reliability → consistent
    
- intimacy → human connection
    
- self-interest ↓ → trust ↑
    

---

👉 Practical:

- small talk
    
- humor
    
- authenticity
    
- listening
    

---

---

🧠 11. EMPATHY IN COMMUNICATION

---

Principle:

```text
Assume good intent
```

---

Reality:

- difficult stakeholder ≠ bad person
    
- may have:
    
    - stress
        
    - pressure
        
    - personal issues
        

---

👉 Insight:

```text
Understand person → unlock collaboration
```

---

---

🧠 12. HANDLING CONFLICT

---

Approach:

```text
Capture → Structure → Compare → Decide
```

---

Example:

- document both sides
    
- show impact
    
- escalate if needed
    

---

👉 Insight:

```text
BA = facilitator, not judge
```
