
---

# 🧠 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Data alone does NOT drive decisions → ==**storytelling is required to create impact**==
    
- A strong data story = **Problem → Root Cause → Action → ROI → Time → Risk**
    
- The goal is NOT to show analysis → but to **drive a decision**
    
- Audience matters: storytelling must adapt to **decision maker’s metrics**
    
- AI increases need for storytelling → **human context + narrative = critical layer**
    

---

# 🔍 2. KEY INSIGHTS / PROBLEMS

## 🔥 Core problem

> Analysts focus on **analysis output**, not **decision impact**

- Too many slides, too much data
    
- No clear recommendation
    
- No urgency → no action
    

---

## 🔥 ==Data without story = useless==

> “Numbers are forgettable, stories stick”

- Raw data = no context
    
- No frame of reference → stakeholders don’t act
    

---

## 🔥 Common BA mistakes (VERY RELEVANT)

- ❌ Showing “how the sausage is made” (too much technical detail)
    
- ❌ No executive summary
    
- ❌ Data not aligned with “source of truth”
    
- ❌ No stakeholder alignment before presenting
    
- ❌ Over-reliance on tools (PowerBI, etc.)
    

---

## 🔥 Real goal of BA (VERY IMPORTANT FOR YOU)

> Your job = **influence decision**, not present data

## 🧠 3.1 DATA STORYTELLING FRAMEWORK (CORE)

### Step 0 — Define context

- What decision are you driving?
    
- Who is the decision maker?
    

---

### Step 1 — Problem

- What is happening?
    
- Why does it matter?
    

---

### Step 2 — Root Cause

- What drives the issue?
    

---

### Step 3 — Solution Options

- Option A
    
- Option B
    

---

### Step 4 — Impact (CRITICAL)

- ROI
    
- Business impact
    

---

### Step 5 — Time

- When can it be implemented?
    
- When results appear?
    

---

### Step 6 — Risk

- Execution risks
    
- Dependencies
    

---

👉 This is your **BRD + Decision layer**

---

## ⚙️ 3.2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (MUST USE)

Format:

```text
Problem → Root Cause → Action → Timing
```

Example:

```text
Sales are down in Southeast.
Root cause: competitor promotion.
Action: launch counter-promo.
Timeline: next quarter.
```

👉 This is your **elevator pitch**

---

## 🧩 3.3 STORY STRUCTURE (MENTAL MODEL)

```text
Data = Map
Story = Journey
Stakeholder = Destination
```

👉 Very powerful for your AI / BA role

## 🧠 3.4 STAKEHOLDER ADAPTATION (IMPORTANT)

---

### Non-technical stakeholders:

- Focus: business metrics (revenue, growth)
    
- Output: impact, ROI
    

---

### Technical stakeholders:

- Focus: model performance
    
- Output: drivers, logic
    

---

👉 Rule:

> Always speak in **their KPI language**

---

## ⚙️ 3.5 VISUALIZATION RULES (VERY PRACTICAL)

---

### Golden rule:

> “3-second rule”

If user cannot understand in 3 seconds → BAD chart

---

### Key principles:

- Simplicity > beauty
    
- Insight = hero (not chart)
    
- Use correct chart:
    
    - Bar → comparison
        
    - Line → trend
        
    - Scatter → correlation
        

---

### Avoid:

- Clutter
    
- Too many categories in pie chart
    
- Long numbers (use 5M instead of 5,000,000)
    

---
