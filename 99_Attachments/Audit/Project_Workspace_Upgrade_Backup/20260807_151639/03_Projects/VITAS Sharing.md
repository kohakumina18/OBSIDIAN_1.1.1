`VITAS.Sharing.202606` is an external sharing / communication initiative about PPJ Group's practical AI, automation, and digital transformation journey in textile manufacturing. It is not a normal delivery project; it exists to structure the PPJ story, case studies, lessons learned, and governance messages for external sharing.

The content should focus on real PPJ use cases across ERP workflows, invoice automation, sourcing data, FD/CPD datamarts, chatbots, agents, dashboards, factory data, and adoption governance. Sensitive customer, ERP/API, and internal system details must not be exposed.

Next work is to select 3-5 representative case studies, prepare slides/one-pager, confirm what can be shared externally, review with management, and capture follow-up feedback.
