# Project Knowledge Detail

## Executive Summary

`QC.Primo1D.RFID.Thread.v1.0` is an exploration of Primo1D RFID thread technology for product identification, tracking, traceability, anti-mix-up, QC support, production tracking, and linking products to digital records.

Current status is Exploration, around 5%. The next work is to clarify the business use case, where RFID would be attached/read in the process, reader/device requirements, data model, cost/benefit, and whether a PoC is justified.

Main risks are high cost, unclear integration, weak use case, unclear reading point in the process, and possible impact on garment/product handling.
