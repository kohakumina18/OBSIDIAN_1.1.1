# 🧠 3.4 REQUIREMENTS + QA (VERY IMPORTANT)

---

## Problem:

```text

```

👉 Each step = translation error risk

## AI Solution:

- detect missing info
    
- paraphrase requirement

### 2. Traceability


AI can:

- detect missing links
    
- suggest gaps
    

---

### 3. Defect analysis

- analyze production issues
    
- find root cause
    
- identify weak requirements
    

---

👉 Insight:

```text
60% defects = requirement/design phase
```
