– What Companies Actually Look For (Skills, Hiring & Career Strategy)
---

🧠 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Agile organizations need **people who adapt to change, not just follow process**
    
- Hiring is shifting toward:
    
    - **communication + adaptability + domain awareness**
        
- Biggest mismatch:
    
    - companies want “unicorns” (BA + Scrum + PM + domain expert)
        
- Real success = balance between:
    
    - core BA skill
        
    - domain knowledge
        
    - collaboration ability
        
- Career growth = **self-awareness + positioning + continuous adaptation**
