

# 📄 Agile BA Hiring – What Companies Really Want vs What You Think They Want

---

🧠 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- “Agile organization” = **organization adapting to speed of change**
    
- Hiring trend:
    
    - NOT just “good BA”
        
    - BUT “BA + domain + adaptability”
        
- Biggest gap:
    
    - companies want **unicorns**
        
    - market doesn’t have them
        
- Real winning strategy:
    
    - strong core BA skills
        
    - - domain positioning
            
    - - communication & mindset
            

---

🔍 KEY INSIGHTS / PROBLEMS

---

🔥 Agile = reaction to change

```text
Change faster → must deliver faster
```

---

👉 Implication:

```text
Long analysis = waste
```

---

🔥 Hiring expectation problem

```text
BA + Scrum + PM + Domain Expert
```

---

👉 Reality:

```text
Rare + expensive + unrealistic
```

---

🔥 Market mismatch

- companies want niche skills
    
- talent pool shrinking (remote → onsite shift)
    

👉 Result:

```text
Expectation ↓ OR Hiring delay ↑
```

Companies prefer domain-first
