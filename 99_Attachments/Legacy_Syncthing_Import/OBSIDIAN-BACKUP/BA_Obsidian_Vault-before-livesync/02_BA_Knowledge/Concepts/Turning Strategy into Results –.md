BA Perspective & Execution Framework
---

# 🧠 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Strategy fails mainly due to **lack of clarity, no iteration, and poor execution discipline**
    
- Strategy ≠ analysis → strategy = **clear idea of how to win**
    
- BA skills are critical in strategy: **asking why, context analysis, stakeholder alignment**
    
- Execution requires **OKRs + PDCA cycle (Plan–Do–Check–Act)**
    
- Most important human skill: **Resilience (ability to learn through failure and adapt)**
    

---

# 🔍 2. KEY INSIGHTS / PROBLEMS

---

## 🔥 Strategy is misunderstood

> “The result of strategy analysis is NOT strategy”

Common issue:

- Companies create:
    
    - 200-page analysis
        
    - No clear direction
        

👉 Insight:

> Strategy must be **simple, focused, actionable**

---

## 🔥 Root cause of strategy failure

### 1. No clear end goal (Vision problem)

- Don’t know:
    
    - where to win
        
    - what “winning” means
        

---

### 2. No clear strategy (Idea problem)

- No clear:
    
    - direction
        
    - leverage
        
    - prioritization
        

---

### 3. No iteration (Execution problem)

- No learning loop
    
- No feedback cycle
    

---

👉 Formula:

```text
Bad Strategy = No clarity + No choice + No iteration
```

---

## 🔥 Strategy = Continuous adaptation (VERY IMPORTANT)

> Strategy is like chess:

- You move
    
- Competitor reacts
    
- You adjust
    

👉 Insight:

> Strategy is NOT static → it is **dynamic decision-making**

# 📦 3. STRUCTURED CONTENT (READY TO USE)

---

## 🧠 3.1 WHAT IS STRATEGY (CLEAR DEFINITION)

```text
Vision → Strategy → Execution
```

---

### Vision

- What is the end game?
    
- Where do we want to win?
    

---

### Strategy

> Idea of how to win

- Based on:
    
    - market
        
    - customer
        
    - capability
        

---

### Execution

- Actions
    
- Iteration
    
- Learning
    

---

## ⚙️ 3.2 STRATEGY EXECUTION MODEL

```text
Plan → Do → Check → Act → Repeat
```

👉 (PDCA Cycle)

---

### Application:

- Plan → Define hypothesis
    
- Do → Execute
    
- Check → Measure
    
- Act → Adjust
    

---

## 🧠 3.3 OKR FRAMEWORK (FOR YOU – VERY USEFUL)

---

### Definition:

```text
Objective → measured by Key Results
```

---

### Key principles:

- Must be:
    
    - measurable
        
    - actionable
        
    - team-controlled
        

---

### Important distinction:

|Type|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Leading metric|drives result|
|Lagging metric|result outcome|

👉 Example:

- Revenue = lagging
    
- Conversion rate = leading
    

---

## 🧠 3.4 STRATEGY = LEARNING SYSTEM

---

> Strategy is NOT execution  
> Strategy = learning what works

---

### Core loop:

```text
Hypothesis → Experiment → Learn → Adjust
```

---

👉 This is EXACTLY:

- AI system thinking
    
- BA thinking
    
- Startup thinking
    

---

## 🧠 3.5 BA ROLE IN STRATEGY (VERY IMPORTANT FOR YOU)

---

### BA brings:

- Root cause analysis
    
- Context understanding
    
- Stakeholder alignment
    
- Problem definition
    

---

### Key skill:

```text
Ask WHY → go deeper → define real problem
```

---

👉 Example:

Stakeholder says:

> “We want to grow revenue”

BA asks:

- Why?
    
- What is winning?
    
- Which market?
    
- Which customer?
    

---

## 🧠 3.6 CULTURE IMPACT (CRITICAL)

---

> Culture = behaviors we accept

---

### Examples:

- Accept no measurement → strategy fails
    
- Accept no iteration → strategy fails
    
- Accept blame → no learning
    

---

👉 Insight:

> Culture determines if strategy works

---

## 🧠 3.7 RESILIENCE (CORE HUMAN SKILL)

---

### Definition:

> Ability to:

- fail
    
- recover
    
- learn
    
- adapt
    

---

### How to build:

- Fail fast
    
- Learn fast
    
- Drop ego
    
- Iterate
    

---

👉 Key insight:

> You cannot build resilience without failure

Add:

```text

- What is the business “win”?
- What is the hypothesis?
```

---

### 2. For projects (SmartFlow, AI)

Use:

```text
Hypothesis → Experiment → Result → Decision
```

---

### 3. For leadership communication

Always clarify:

- What is winning?
    
- What is priority?
    
- What is next move?
    

---

Example:

```text

Reduce invoice processing time

Key Results:
- Time < 5s per file
- Error rate < 2%
```
