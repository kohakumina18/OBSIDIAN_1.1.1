# Project Knowledge Detail

## Executive Summary

`PPJ.UIT.ACADEMIC.COLLABORATION.v1.1` is an academic collaboration initiative between PPJ Group and the Faculty of Information Systems at UIT. It connects real PPJ business problems with academic activities such as graduation thesis topics, course projects, research groups, academic contests, mentoring, and student prototyping.

UIT has responded positively to the collaboration direction and is open to reviewing PPJ real-world problems for suitable academic formats.

## Business Context

PPJ has many practical AI, automation, data, chatbot, dashboard, sourcing, costing, datamart, and manufacturing process problems that can be converted into smaller research/prototype topics. The collaboration should create an innovation pipeline without exposing sensitive data or oversized business problems to students.

## Objectives

- Connect PPJ real business problems with UIT students and lecturers.
- Build a pipeline of ideas, prototypes, research, and applied innovation.
- Support PPJ exploration of new AI/data/process automation use cases.
- Improve PPJ visibility in academic and innovation communities.
- Create internship, recruitment, mentoring, and talent pipeline opportunities.

## Scope

### In Scope

- General PPJ x UIT collaboration discussion.
- Topic selection for thesis, course project, research group, mentoring, prototype, or contest use.
- AISC participation review as sponsor, judge, mentor, or real-business problem provider.
- Exploration of a PPJ-themed academic contest if format, faculty resources, criteria, and organization model are clear.
- Preparation of short, clear PPJ problem statements with business context and expected output.

### Out of Scope

- Sharing sensitive production, ERP, customer, financial, or confidential data without masking/NDA.
- Giving students problems too large to complete.
- Treating academic prototypes as production-ready systems.
- Committing PPJ to sponsorship, contest, or data sharing without internal approval.

## Next Actions

- Summarize PPJ problem topics suitable for UIT.

## Risks and Dependencies

- Problems must be practical but safe to share.
- Data masking or NDA may be needed for company data.
- Oversized topics need decomposition before student use.
- Ownership of research/prototype output must be clarified.
- PPJ needs an internal owner for each topic.

## Do Not Drift Rules

- Do not classify this as a vendor solution project.
- Do not place it under QSee/NUNOX/Stratova vendor evaluation cluster.
- Do not expose sensitive PPJ/customer/system data.
- Do not treat academic prototypes as production systems.
