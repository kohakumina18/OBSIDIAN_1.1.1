# 🧠 1. THE CORE MODEL (WHAT THIS WHOLE THING IS REALLY ABOUT)

> **Process Improvement = People × Process × Perception × Culture**

Most analysts fail because they only optimize:

- ❌ Process (steps, flow)
    
- ❌ Data (metrics, KPIs)
    

But ignore:

- ✅ People (fear, habits, incentives)
    
- ✅ Perception (what is “a problem” differs)
    
- ✅ Culture (history of change)
    

👉 This is explicitly shown throughout the discussion

---

# 🔥 2. FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPTS (YOU MUST UNDERSTAND THESE)

---

## 2.1 Resistance ≠ “People hate change”

### ✔ Real definition:

> Resistance = **Protection mechanism**

People resist because:

- “It already works (for me)”
    
- Fear of:
    
    - losing control
        
    - losing competence
        
    - unknown future
        
- No clarity:
    
    - what will change?
        
    - how it affects me?
        

👉 Key insight:

> Resistance is rational from their perspective

---

## 2.2 “Problem is relative”

### Two realities:

- Leadership: sees KPI / cost / P&L problem
    
- Operators: see “daily work is fine”
    

👉 Result:

> Same process → different truths

---

## 2.3 The SME (Subject Matter Expert) principle

### Who is the real expert?

- NOT the analyst
    
- NOT the manager
    

👉 It is:

> The person doing the job daily

### How to identify SME:

> Whoever describes the most real problems

---

## 2.4 Influence & group dynamics

### Concept:

- Rooms are influenced by:
    
    - strong personalities
        
    - informal leaders
        

👉 If 1 key person resists:

- entire room resists (groupthink)
    

👉 If converted:

- entire room aligns
    

---

## 2.5 Organizational “Change Trauma”

### Concept:

- Past failed initiatives → emotional memory
    

Symptoms:

- “We tried this before”
    
- “This won’t work”
    
- passive resistance
    

👉 Called:

> **Transformation PTSD**

---

## 2.6 BA role = “The Middle Layer”

You are:

- NOT decision maker
    
- NOT executor
    

👉 You are:

> Translator between strategy ↔ execution

# ⚠️ 3. CRITICAL MISTAKES (VERY IMPORTANT)

---

## ❌ Mistake 1: Acting like the expert

- “I know how to fix this”
    

👉 Impact:

- triggers defensiveness
    

✔ Correct:

> “You know the process, I know how to improve it”

---

## ❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring people side

- focusing only on efficiency
    

👉 Impact:

- resistance increases
    

---

## ❌ Mistake 3: Overusing frameworks (Lean Six Sigma)

- DMAIC, Kaizen, Fishbone, etc.
    

👉 Impact:

- overwhelm users
    
- create barrier
    

✔ Correct:

> Use frameworks internally, simplify externally

---

## ❌ Mistake 4: Not identifying influencers

- ignoring:
    
    - silent expert
        
    - “bully in the room”
        

👉 Impact:

- hidden resistance blocks progress
    

---

## ❌ Mistake 5: Taking ownership

- BA becomes:
    
    - process owner
        
    - executor
        

👉 Impact:

- solution dies when BA leaves
    

✔ Correct:

> Stakeholders must own the solution

---

## ❌ Mistake 6: Poor communication from leadership

- change pushed without:
    
    - explanation
        
    - roadmap
        

👉 Impact:

- confusion + distrust
    

---

# ⚙️ 4. KEY METHODS (HOW TO DO IT PROPERLY)

---

## 4.1 Discovery method (VERY PRACTICAL)

Instead of:

- “Here’s the problem”
    

Do:

- “How is your process going?”
    
- “What issues do YOU see?”
    

👉 Let them speak first

---

## 4.2 Problem alignment method

You must align:

|Source|Type|
|---|---|
|People|Experience|
|Data|Evidence|

👉 Formula:

> Truth = Experience + Data

---

## 4.3 Stakeholder engagement method

Steps:

1. Identify:
    
    - SMEs
        
    - influencers
        
    - resistors
        
2. Engage:
    
    - allies → group discussion
        
    - resistors → 1-on-1
        
3. Convert:
    
    - especially strong personalities
        

---

## 4.4 Communication method

Adapt message:

|Audience|Focus|
|---|---|
|Leadership|ROI, metrics|
|Middle|execution|
|Operators|daily impact|

---

## 4.5 Change enablement method

Must answer:

- WHY change?
    
- WHAT change?
    
- HOW change?
    

👉 If missing → resistance

---

## 4.6 Leadership alignment method

- Bring leader to:
    
    - announce change
        
    - explain purpose
        

👉 This creates:

> legitimacy + trust

---

## 4.7 Process mapping method

### Use layered approach:

|Level|Example|
|---|---|
|High|Order → Ship|
|Mid|Procurement → Production|
|Low|Send email|

👉 Rule:

> Only go deeper if it affects the problem

---

## 4.8 Ownership design method

- Assign ownership early
    

👉 Principle:

> “If they don’t own it → they won’t sustain it”
