# Project Knowledge Detail

## Executive Summary

`MER.MARKET.INTELLIGENCE.v1.1` analyzes product, market, and consumer signals to support MER/business decisions around product opportunities, fitting, color, material, fabric groups, and possible reboost/showcase candidates. It combines internal data with external market reference such as Quince consumer data.

Internal data may include sales history, customer/PO data, inventory fabric, product/item data, and historical samples. External reference may include Quince product data, price points, review/rating, descriptions, colors, silhouettes, fit, and material/fabric trends.

Expected outputs include market intelligence insight report, product opportunity list, fabric reboost suggestions, showcase candidates, and a data-backed proposal for MER/business users.
