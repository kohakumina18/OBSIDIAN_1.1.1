# 03  PHILOSOPHY & CRITICAL THINKING

Self-discipline vs Freedom  |  Kỷ luật giúp ta tự do hay giới hạn lựa chọn?

## Knowledge / Insight

Self-discipline is the ability to act in line with longer-term goals even when short-term desires pull us in another direction. Freedom can mean having choices, but it can also mean being able to act on the choices we value.

Kỷ luật là khả năng hành động theo mục tiêu lâu dài thay vì chỉ chạy theo mong muốn trước mắt. Tự do vừa là có quyền lựa chọn, vừa là khả năng thực hiện điều mình coi trọng.

|                                                                     |                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **View A – Discipline creates freedom**                             | **View B – Discipline can restrict freedom**                       |
| A routine may reduce decision fatigue and help people pursue goals. | Rigid rules may create guilt or reduce enjoyment of everyday life. |
| Example: Planning meals makes a busy week easier.                   | Example: Refusing all social meals to follow a strict routine.     |

## Applying – Mini debate

Scenario: A student follows a strict diet and exercise schedule but often turns down invitations from friends. Is this self-discipline or a loss of freedom?

**My position + reason**

**A reasonable counterargument**

**My revised view / condition**

## Transfer to Speaking Part 3

To some extent, … because … . For example, … . However, this depends on … .

Discuss: Should SOCIETY / PEOPLE encourage healthy habits without telling OTHERS exactly what to eat?

# 08  SPEAKING PART 3

Analytical answers  |  Claim → Reason → Example → Qualification

## Knowledge / Insight – Extend without repeating

Q: Why do some people struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle?  
Model: One reason is that healthy choices can require time and planning. For instance, people who work long shifts may buy convenient meals rather than cook. That said, the problem is not always a lack of time; habits and the availability of affordable options also matter.

# 09  LANGUAGE & CORRECTION LAB

Paraphrase, discourse markers, fluency and evidence-based feedback

## A. Paraphrase – Giữ nguyên ý, không chỉ đổi từ

1. Many office workers spend most of the day sitting. (Use: sedentary lifestyle)

2. People can reduce the amount of sugar they consume. (Use: cut down on)

3. Clear food labels help people choose more carefully. (Use: make informed choices)

4. Healthy eating matters, but it should not make people feel guilty. (Use: That said)

## B. Recover naturally when speaking

Mix up two words = nhầm hai từ; lose your train of thought = quên mạch ý; get tongue-tied = nói vấp vì lúng túng. Các idioms mô tả lỗi, không cần nhét vào bài thi.

Repair drill: Say “I eat less snacks …”, then correct yourself using “Sorry, I mean …”. Practise stressing FEWER, then continue the answer without restarting.
