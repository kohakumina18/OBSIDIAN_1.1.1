# Knowledge linking and Second Brain retrieval

## Procedure
1. Search topic names, aliases, properties, outgoing links, and backlinks across relevant parts of the vault.
2. Distinguish canonical notes, supporting evidence, dated events, decisions, and reusable concepts.
3. Describe relationships as `supports`, `depends on`, `contradicts`, `derived from`, or `next action` rather than indiscriminately linking every mention.
4. Suggest a Map of Content (MOC) with 5–12 well-chosen notes if it materially improves retrieval.
5. Point out orphan notes, duplicates and unresolved/ambiguous concepts as **proposals**, not automatic changes.
6. Only insert links after verifying target notes, or explicitly identify proposed note stubs.

## Output
- Short synthesis of the knowledge cluster.
- Proposed MOC structure and link recommendations.
- Important gaps or conflicting sources.
- If updates were authorized, exact modified-file list.
