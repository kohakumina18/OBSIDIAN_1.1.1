# Meeting synthesis

## Procedure
1. Read all supplied transcript or source-note content before drawing conclusions; preserve business terminology.
2. Extract stakeholders, meeting context, objective, current process, pain points, systems/data, suggestions, confirmed decisions, requirements, risks, dependencies, open questions and next steps.
3. Consolidate repeated points but retain material conflicts or changes of position.
4. Explicitly label **Confirmed Decision**, **Proposal**, **Requirement to Validate**, **Assumption**, **Open Question**.
5. Produce a concise meeting note with action table: action | owner | due date | evidence/status. Write `TBD` for missing owner or deadline.
6. Where appropriate, link to existing project/requirements notes; do not overwrite verbatim transcript or replace evidence with invented detail.

## Quality gate
- Can a stakeholder tell which points were actually agreed?
- Can a BA turn every open point into a follow-up question?
- Are source notes and conflicting statements still traceable?
